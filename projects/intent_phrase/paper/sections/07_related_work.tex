\section{Background and related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Joint-embedding prediction.}
JEPA was proposed as a route to predictive representations that avoid reconstructing every detail of an observation \citep{lecun2022path}. I-JEPA predicts representations of masked image regions from visible context \citep{assran2023ijepa}. V-JEPA extends feature prediction to video and studies motion-sensitive representations \citep{bardes2024vjepa}. V-JEPA 2 combines large-scale action-free video pretraining with an action-conditioned model for robot planning toward image goals \citep{assran2025vjepa2}. These works motivate latent prediction, but they do not imply that metric distance ranks discrete language actions.

Language-focused JEPA work is emerging. LLM-JEPA combines latent prediction with language-model training \citep{huang2025llmjepa}. JEPA-Reasoner studies reasoning through latent predictive objectives \citep{jepareasoner2026}. Our scope is narrower. We study controlled language actions with exact consequences and within-state counterfactual orderings. The resulting experiment separates action proposal, transition prediction, and candidate ranking.

\paragraph{Planning-oriented representation shaping.}
Several recent methods address the mismatch between prediction and planning. Value-guided JEPA shapes embedding distance to approximate a goal-conditioned reaching value \citep{destrade2026value}. Temporal straightening reduces latent-trajectory curvature so Euclidean distance better approximates planning-relevant geodesics \citep{wang2026straightening}. Temporal-Distance JEPA learns a directed temporal cost using trajectory order, cross-trajectory negatives, and rollout consistency \citep{bai2026tdjepa}. GAR shares the goal of making latent structure useful for action selection. It instead imposes local within-state order constraints over discrete language actions and trains an amortized score that factors through predicted successors.

Direct numerical comparison with visual continuous-control planners would not be meaningful because observations, action spaces, supervision, and evaluation differ. We do need matched conceptual controls. Raw distance, detached geometry, direct ranking, and exact transition diagnostics test whether our result is due to metric shaping, a supervised readout, or predictive dynamics. A temporal-order or straightening auxiliary on iGSM becomes necessary if we claim empirical advantage over those objectives. The present paper does not make that claim.

\paragraph{Latent world models and planning.}
Latent dynamics have long supported model-based control. Dreamer learns compact recurrent state-space models for imagined policy learning \citep{hafner2020dreamer}. MuZero learns dynamics, reward, policy, and value representations without reconstructing observations \citep{schrittwieser2020muzero}. These systems demonstrate that a predictive state can be decision useful when policy and value losses shape it. GAR can be understood as an action-level ordering objective for an analogous language-action setting, with exact counterfactual transitions available for diagnosis.

\paragraph{Test-time computation.}
Autoregressive reasoning often allocates more inference computation through longer traces, sampling, search, or verification \citep{snell2024scaling}. Weight-shared recurrent Transformers permit depth to vary without increasing parameters. The recurrent-depth recipe of \citet{geiping2025recurrent} samples loop counts during training and evaluates extrapolation at inference. JEPA rollouts offer another route because latent states can be simulated without decoding full text. Our repaired negative result shows that this potential depends on rollout stability and value calibration.

\paragraph{Controlled reasoning environments.}
iGSM generates arithmetic dependency graphs with controlled reasoning complexity \citep{igsm2025}. ProofWriter provides natural-language rule reasoning and explicit proofs \citep{tafjord2021proofwriter}. PlanBench translates classical planning tasks into language while retaining executable symbolic validation \citep{valmeekam2023planbench}. ALFWorld connects language instructions with interactive household environments \citep{shridhar2021alfworld}. These environments offer complementary tests of arithmetic, deduction, planning, and grounded interaction. The current empirical claims are restricted to stylized iGSM until the remaining adapters pass their validity gates.
