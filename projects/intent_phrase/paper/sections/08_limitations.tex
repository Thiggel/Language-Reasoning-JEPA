\section{Limitations and scope}

The main interface supplies the current symbolic feasible-action menu, isolating action selection from unrestricted proposal and language generation. The reported task is conditional planning among executable phrases rather than autonomous language agency.

The completed evidence comes from a stylized arithmetic environment. Templated language and exact counterfactual transitions enable mechanism analysis, but they limit external validity. No result establishes transfer to faithful iGSM or a non-arithmetic domain.

Several mechanism controls use one seed. Direct GAR matches transition-value GAR in that setting. Detached gradients, raw-distance planning, and the local metric audit therefore remain exploratory and do not support population-level comparisons.

The deeper-planning protocol does not support a test-time-scaling claim because proposal work, recursive prediction error, and recurrent-baseline FLOPs are not yet jointly controlled. No scaling curve is reported.

The present model is trained from scratch on a controlled language. Its findings do not directly establish how pretrained large language models would organize open-domain planning states. Conversely, the controlled setting enables exact causal comparisons that would be difficult to interpret in an open-ended agent benchmark.
